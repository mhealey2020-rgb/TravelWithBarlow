The Story

Hi, I’m Barlow

Travel planner, professional over-packer, and your loudest cheerleader at the airport gate.

Travel with Barlow started with one simple belief: everybody deserves a trip that feels like the season finale of their favorite show — big entrances, better exits, and a story you’ll be telling at brunch for years.

I’ve always been the friend with the color-coded itinerary and the secret dinner reservation. Somewhere between planning group getaways and talking my way into the best table in town, I realized this was more than a hobby — it was a calling. So I turned it into Travel with Barlow: a travel-planning experience built for the LGBTQ+ community and anyone who wants their vacation served with a little extra sparkle.

I plan trips the way the Housewives throw a party — intentionally, dramatically, and with zero tolerance for a boring moment. But underneath the glitter is real care: safe and welcoming destinations, stays that actually celebrate who you are, and details handled so thoroughly that all you have to do is show up and shine.

What I’m all about

  • Everyone’s invited. Queer, questioning, allies, chosen family — this table has room for all of us.
  • Safety with style. I vet destinations and stays so you can relax and be fully yourself.
  • Personal, not templated. Your trip is built around your vibe — honeymoon, girls’ trip, solo reinvention, or all three.
  • Big fun, real substance. The parties are unforgettable, and so are the sunsets, the food, and the quiet moments in between.
